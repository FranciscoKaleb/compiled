1 · The victim: your profile

You need a cookie session first.

Signed in as . Contact email:

2 · The defence

When on, the server issues a random token tied to your session; the legitimate form includes it in a hidden field. The attacker\'s form cannot know it.

3 · The attack

Open the attacker\'s page. It looks like a prize draw. It contains a hidden form pointing at /lab/api/auth/profile that submits itself on load — and your browser attaches your session cookie because it always does.

Open attacker page ↗

Come back here and re-check. With the defence off your email is now hacked@evil.example. Turn it on and try again.
